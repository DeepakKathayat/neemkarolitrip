Nainital and Kathgodam to Kainchi Dham

Last checked 18 October 2026

Kainchi Dham is on the Nainital–Almora road, 9 km past Bhowali. Here is how far it is from each nearby town, and what to know about the last stretch.

Short answer

  • From Kathgodam station: about 40–43 km, roughly 1–1.5 hours by taxi.
  • From Nainital: 17 km, about 40 minutes.
  • On busy weekends you may have to park at Bhimtal or Bhowali and take the shuttle.

Bhowali, Nainital, Kathgodam and Pantnagar distances come from the District Nainital website. The Haldwani figure is an approximate road distance.

From Kathgodam

  • Kathgodam is the nearest railway station. Taxis are available outside the station, and the ride takes roughly 1–1.5 hours. A bus ride is a little over an hour.
  • The road goes through Bhimtal or Bhowali, then onto the Nainital–Almora road.
  • Agree the fare before you start, and ask the driver whether the current traffic plan lets taxis go all the way. On recent busy weekends, taxis were not allowed into the Kainchi Dham area.
  • Train timings from Delhi are on our Delhi to Kainchi Dham page.

From Nainital

  • It is 17 km, about 40 minutes by road, via Bhowali.
  • On busy weekends, vehicles coming via Gethia or Nainital have been parked at the Sanatorium in Bhowali, with a shuttle onward. Start early.
  • Nainital also works as a base. You can visit Kainchi Dham in the morning and see the lake in the afternoon. See places nearby.

From Haldwani and Pantnagar

  • Haldwani is a gateway town, about 40–45 km away. Buses and taxis run from here.
  • Pantnagar airport is about 79 km away. A sedan taxi costs roughly ₹1,800 one way.
  • Visitors from Haldwani and Kathgodam were sent to park at Bhimtal and Vikas Bhawan during the June fair.

Where to stay nearby

The ashram does not generally host the public overnight. Most visitors stay in Bhowali, Bhimtal or Nainital. Book ahead on busy weekends. See where to stay, and the timings and etiquette guide before you go.

Common questions

How far is Kainchi Dham from Kathgodam?

About 40–43 km. A taxi takes roughly 1–1.5 hours.

How far is Kainchi Dham from Nainital?

17 km, about 40 minutes by road.

What is the nearest railway station to Kainchi Dham?

Kathgodam, about 40–43 km away.

Can I take a taxi from Kathgodam to the temple on a weekend?

It depends on that weekend's traffic plan. In recent plans, taxis and two-wheelers were not allowed into the Kainchi Dham area, and visitors parked at Bhimtal or Bhowali and took the shuttle. Ask your driver and check Latest rules.

What is the nearest airport to Kainchi Dham?

Pantnagar, about 79 km away. Bareilly is also listed as a nearby airport.
