Kainchi Dham timings and etiquette

Last checked 18 October 2026

What time to go, how long darshan takes, and the simple rules to follow inside the temple.

Short answer

  • Opening hours: generally about 6 AM to 7 PM. Hours and aarti times change with the season and on festival days.
  • Best time: a weekday morning between 6 and 8 AM, when it is calmest.
  • No photos inside the temple, and no entry fee.

Opening hours and aarti

Government tourism listings give the opening time as 6:00 AM and the closing time as 7:00 PM. Hours and aarti times change with the season and on festival days, so check the notices of the Shri Kainchi Hanuman Mandir & Ashram Trust before a special day. We are an independent guide and cannot confirm exact aarti times.

Best time of day and week

  • Weekday mornings, 6 to 8 AM, are the calmest. Darshan takes about 1–2 hours including the queue.
  • Weekends can take several hours, plus shuttle time. On 4 October the queue began at 4 AM and darshan opened at 7 AM.
  • On busy days, plan for parking at Bhimtal or Bhowali and a shuttle. See parking and shuttle rules.

Etiquette: what to do and not do

  • No photography inside the temple. Keep your phone away during darshan.
  • Dress simply and modestly. You will leave footwear outside.
  • Carry a warm layer. The valley is cool even on summer evenings.
  • No overnight stay inside the ashram. It generally does not host the public overnight. Book a room in Bhowali, Bhimtal or Nainital.
  • Darshan is free. Small costs are the shuttle, parking and any prasad you buy. We never sell darshan, puja or prasad.
  • No picnics on the Shipra riverbank.

Best months to visit

  • March to June and September to November have the best weather.
  • 15 June is the Foundation Day fair. Avoid it unless you want the fair, because crowds are very large.
  • July to September can bring landslides and road closures. Check the weather and road status before driving up.

Common questions

What time does Kainchi Dham open?

Generally about 6 AM, and it closes at about 7 PM. Hours and aarti times change with the season and on festival days, so check the trust's notices.

How long does darshan take?

About 1–2 hours on a weekday morning, including the queue. On weekends it can take several hours, plus shuttle time.

Is photography allowed inside Kainchi Dham?

No. Photography is not allowed inside the temple. Keep your phone away during darshan.

Is there an entry fee?

No. Darshan is free. Small costs are the shuttle, parking and any prasad you buy.

What is the best time to visit Kainchi Dham?

March to June and September to November for weather, and weekday mornings for the shortest queues. Avoid 15 June unless you want the fair.
