Kainchi Dham parking and shuttle rules

Last checked 18 October 2026

Parking and shuttle rules change almost every busy weekend. This page explains how the system usually works and shows a real example, so you know what to expect. For the newest plan, always check Latest rules on our home page.

Short answer

  • Quiet weekdays: you can usually drive to the parking near the temple.
  • Busy weekends, holidays and fair days: police send private vehicles to set parking lots at Bhimtal or Bhowali, and you continue by shuttle.
  • Shuttle fares and timings were not published in the plans we saw. Ask at the parking lot.

How it works

When crowds are expected, Nainital Police and the district administration announce a traffic plan. Vehicles stop at designated parking lots, and shuttle buses and cabs carry visitors to the temple. The shuttle already ran during the June fair, and the administration said it would run on weekends and festive days. From the parking spots, visitors continue by taxi, roadways bus or Kumaon Mandal Vikas Nigam (KMVN) bus.

Example: the 17–18 October 2026 weekend

Nainital Police announced this plan for Saturday 17 and Sunday 18 October (as reported by Amar Ujala on 16 October). It is only an example and has now passed.

  • Hours: 5 AM to 8 PM, for KMU buses, private buses and four-wheelers.
  • Coming via Bhimtal (from outside the district and state): park at the Vikas Bhawan parking in Bhimtal, then take the shuttle to Bhowali and Kainchi Dham.
  • Coming via Gethia or Nainital: park at the Sanatorium parking in Bhowali, then take the shuttle to the temple.
  • Taxis and two-wheelers were not allowed in the Kainchi Dham area and Nainital that weekend.
  • Through traffic to Almora, Jageshwar, Bageshwar and Pithoragarh was diverted via Bhimtal, Dhari and Dhanachuli.

Earlier plans (for comparison)

  • 10–11 October: cars via Bhimtal parked at Vikas Bhawan in Bhimtal, and cars via Gethia or Nainital at the Sanatorium in Bhowali, with shuttles to the temple. Heavy vehicles were banned from 3 AM to 11 PM, and taxis and two-wheelers were not allowed into the Kainchi Dham area.
  • Foundation Day fair (June 2026): visitors from Haldwani and Kathgodam parked at Bhimtal and Vikas Bhawan. Visitors from Nainital and Ramnagar parked at Metropole and DSA Flats. Shuttle buses and cabs ran from there.

The pattern is the same each time: park at the edge, then take the shuttle. Only the exact lots and dates change.

How to plan around it

  • Choose a weekday if you can. On busy days since the film Hanuman Ansh, visitors reached 35,000–40,000 a day, and on 4 October the queue began at 4 AM.
  • Check Latest rules the day before. The plan is usually announced a day or two ahead.
  • Book your room in advance. The administration asked this, because people searching for rooms after arriving add to the jams.
  • Allow extra time for the shuttle wait and the queue.
  • Ask for the rate list at hotels, homestays and shops. They must now display rates, and overcharging can be reported.
  • No picnics on the Shipra riverbank. Officials have said they will act against it.
  • Registration: none was required at our last check. The Nainital DM and SSP plan to recommend weekend registration with a daily limit, but no start date has been announced. If it starts, register only on the official government portal.

Common questions

Can I take my car to Kainchi Dham?

On quiet weekdays you can drive to the parking near the temple. On busy weekends, holidays and fair days, police send private vehicles to set parking lots, and you continue by shuttle.

Where do I park for Kainchi Dham on a busy weekend?

In the plan for 17–18 October 2026, vehicles coming via Bhimtal parked at Vikas Bhawan in Bhimtal, and vehicles coming via Gethia or Nainital parked at the Sanatorium in Bhowali. Lots can change, so check Latest rules before you leave.

Are taxis and bikes allowed near Kainchi Dham on weekends?

In recent weekend plans, taxis and two-wheelers were not allowed into the Kainchi Dham area. Check the latest plan, because it can change.

What does the shuttle cost?

The traffic plans we saw did not publish a fare or timings. Ask at the parking lot when you arrive.
