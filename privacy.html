In short

  • You don't need to sign up, and we don't ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • The trip planner and "Ask" box run inside your own browser. What you type is not sent to us.
  • We use Google Analytics only if you tap Accept. If you tap Reject, no analytics runs.
  • Some booking links may earn us a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Who we are

neemkarolitrip.com ("Neem Karoli Trip") is an independent travel guide to Kainchi Dham, run from India. We are not connected with the Shri Kainchi Hanuman Mandir & Ashram Trust or any government body, and we never sell darshan, puja or prasad.

What is stored on your device

  • Language choice (nkt-lang): remembers Hindi or English. It stays in your browser.
  • Light or dark mode (nkt-theme): remembers the look you picked with the ☀️/🌙 button.
  • Saved trip plans and recent questions (nkt-plans, nkt-recent, nkt-save): only if you tap Allow and save. They stay on your phone, are never sent to us, and you can remove them anytime with Delete all saved data in the trip planner.
  • Analytics choice (nkt-consent): remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics, so we don't ask again.
  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*): set only after you tap Accept.

Google Analytics

If you accept, we use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used, for example how many people tap a hotel or train link. This helps us improve the guide. Google receives your browser details, approximate location (city level) and the pages you view. Google shortens IP addresses in GA4, and we do not use advertising features or share data for ads. Google's own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

You can change your mind at any time:

Fonts

Our fonts load from Google Fonts. Like any website request, this sends your IP address to Google so the font files can be delivered.

Booking and travel links

Hotel links (for example Agoda or Booking.com) and links to IRCTC, redBus and MakeMyTrip take you to other websites. Some of these are affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission if you book, at no extra cost to you. Once you leave our site, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can withdraw consent, ask what data relates to you, and ask for it to be corrected or erased. Because we don't collect your name or contact details, most requests can be handled by tapping "Change analytics choice" above or clearing your browser data. For anything else, message us on any of our channels below.

Children

This guide is meant for adults planning a trip. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Message us on Instagram, YouTube or our WhatsApp Channel. If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
